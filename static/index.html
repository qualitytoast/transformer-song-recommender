Transformer Song Recommender

By Nathan Hung, September 2026

I wrote this Transformer in plain NumPy, including the autograd engine that trains it, and trained it on 5,000 playlists from the Spotify Million Playlist Dataset. Give it a 10-song playlist and it ranks what should play next.

On held-out playlists it scores 0.0330 NDCG@10, which is 6.7 times better than always recommending the most popular songs (0.0049).

Waking the server up. It sleeps when idle on the free tier, so this can take up to a minute.
